💪 Why your body needs exercise

Your body is like a supercar — the more you use it, the better it performs! Kids who move for 60 minutes daily are more likely to have stronger bones, better heart health, and improved mood.

💪 Quick Tips for Staying Healthy

  • 🍃 Eat a variety of colorful fruits and vegetables.
  • 💰 Drink water instead of sugary drinks.
  • 💡 Get enough sleep each night.
